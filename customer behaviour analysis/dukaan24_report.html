3. The Diwali wave

Monthly revenue over 18 months. One month is not like the others.

The 3 festive weeks (6-26 Oct 2025) alone brought 695 orders and Rs 19.45 lakh - 12.0% of all revenue in 3 weeks, running 2.8x a normal week. Stock, servers and support all need to be Diwali-ready.

6. So what does Dukaan24 do now?

Analysis is only half the job. Here is the action list this data points to.

Guard the Champions

180 people

Early access, loyalty perks, a human on support. At 31% of revenue, retention here is the whole game.

Win back the At-Risk

223 people

They used to shop often and went quiet. A well-timed "we miss you" offer beats acquiring a stranger.

Show up Sun evening

8-9 PM

Run campaigns, pushes and flash sales in the Sun-evening rush, not on sleepy weekday mornings.

Plan big for Diwali

2.8x volume

Inventory, deals and delivery slots locked in weeks ahead. Hunters will arrive for the sale - give them a reason to stay.
